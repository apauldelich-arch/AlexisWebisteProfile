How I work

Problem first. Technology second.

A practical workflow for understanding what matters, building the smallest useful solution and improving it through feedback.

01

Understand

Map the people, decisions, data and friction in the existing process.

02

Build

Design the information structure and test a focused working prototype.

03

Improve

Gather user feedback, validate outcomes and refine the system.

Delich Consulting

Recognise a problem? Let's design a better workflow.

Practical engagements for technical teams, starting with the operational challenge rather than a predetermined tool.

01 / DIAGNOSE

Workflow Review

For teams losing time between spreadsheets, emails and handovers.

Typical deliverables
  • Current-state process map
  • Prioritised friction points
  • Improvement opportunities and next-step plan
02 / BUILD

Digital Systems & Dashboards

For teams that need a single, clearer operational view.

Typical deliverables
  • Requirements and data model
  • Usable prototype or tailored tool
  • Testing and handover documentation
03 / ENABLE

Applied AI Workflows

For technical teams exploring where AI can help reliably.

Typical deliverables
  • Use-case selection and risk review
  • Practical workflow demonstration
  • Training and adoption guidance

Scope and deliverables are agreed for each engagement; these are proposed services, not fixed packages.
