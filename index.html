About me

Argentine Engineer
based in London

I am Alexis Paul Delich, a Chemical Engineer with a passion for sustainability and the circular economy. My journey has taken me from sugar and mineral purification to being a key player in one of the most promising chemical plastic recycling technologies in Europe.

As Technical Manager at Itero Technologies, I operate and optimize a pilot pyrolysis plant in London, transforming plastic waste into high-value raw materials. Every day I work with data, experiments, and decisions that will impact how the world manages plastic on a global scale.

I firmly believe that technology and artificial intelligence are key tools to accelerate the transition to a cleaner future. I am an AI Champion in my organization, integrating AI into R&D workflows to make faster, more impactful decisions.
